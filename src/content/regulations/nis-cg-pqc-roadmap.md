Published in June 2025 by the NIS Cooperation Group's PQC workstream and endorsed by EU member states. It is the EU-level counterpart to NSA CNSA 2.0: first steps by the end of 2026, high-risk use cases (including data in transit exposed to store-now-decrypt-later) by the end of 2030, and the rest as far as feasible by 2035.
